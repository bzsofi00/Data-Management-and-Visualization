# International Movie Market Analysis

An exploratory analysis of the international movie industry, developed using Tableau as part of the **Data Management and Visualization** course at Copenhagen Business School. The project examines movie genres, production companies, audience ratings and country-level performance to identify patterns and potential opportunities in the global film market.

## Interactive Dashboard

**[Explore the International Movie Market Dashboard on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/views/InternationalMovieMarket/Dashboard1?:language=en-US&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)**

## Project Objectives

The objective of this project was to explore the international movie market and identify factors associated with audience engagement and movie performance. The analysis focuses on four main areas:

- **Genre Popularity and Ratings:** Identify popular movie genres and compare their average audience ratings.
- **Production Company Comparison:** Examine the performance of production companies based on movie ratings.
- **Country-Wise Production Success:** Explore geographical patterns in movie production and audience engagement.
- **Historical Rating Trends:** Analyse how movie ratings have evolved over time.

## Dashboard Overview

The Tableau dashboard brings together several visualisations to explore different dimensions of the international movie market.

| Visualisation | Description |
|---|---|
| Genre Popularity and Ratings | Compares average ratings across the top 10 genres ranked by number of votes. |
| Production Company Comparison | Highlights production companies associated with highly rated movies. |
| Country-Wise Production Success | Maps geographical patterns, using circle size to represent cumulative votes and colour to indicate average ratings. |
| Time Series of Ratings | Shows historical trends in movie ratings over the years. |

Interactive filters allow users to explore the data by year, genre, production company and country.

## Key Findings and Business Recommendations

The analysis suggests several opportunities for companies operating in the international film industry:

- **Prioritise audience-preferred genres:** Genres such as Biography, Animation, Drama and Crime perform well in terms of audience ratings among the most-voted genres.
- **Benchmark successful production companies:** Companies such as Miramax Films and Twentieth Century Fox provide useful reference points for understanding production performance.
- **Target promising international markets:** The United States and the United Kingdom, alongside other high-performing countries, represent important markets to consider for distribution and investment.
- **Consider genre-specific market opportunities:** Identifying which countries perform well for particular genres can help inform local production and distribution strategies.
- **Monitor changing audience preferences:** Historical rating trends can provide context for adapting content and investment decisions over time.

Overall, the recommended strategy is to combine genre-level audience insights, production company benchmarking and geographical market analysis when making decisions about film production, investment and international distribution.
