# Data Management and Visualization

A collection of academic projects completed as part of the **Data Management and Visualization (CDSCO2402U)** course at Copenhagen Business School. The projects focus on data preparation, exploratory analysis, database management, interactive Tableau dashboards and translating data into actionable business insights.

## Projects

### 1. International Movie Market Analysis

An analysis of the international movie industry, exploring genre popularity, audience ratings, production companies and geographical market performance.

The project uses Tableau to identify patterns in audience engagement and movie performance, with the aim of supporting strategic decisions related to film production, investment and international distribution.

**Key areas:**
- Analysis of movie genres based on audience votes and average ratings.
- Comparison of production companies and their movie ratings.
- Geographical analysis of movie production and audience engagement.
- Historical trends in movie ratings.
- Business recommendations for production and international market strategy.

**Tools:** Tableau, Data Visualization, Exploratory Analysis

### 2. Copenhagen Airbnb Market Analysis

An analysis of Airbnb's market performance in Copenhagen, focusing on host performance, guest satisfaction, neighbourhood characteristics and opportunities for business growth.

The project combines data preparation, exploratory analysis and PostgreSQL database management with Tableau dashboard development to provide actionable insights for management, operational and business development teams.

**Key areas:**
- Data cleaning, transformation and exploratory analysis of Airbnb listings.
- Database creation and management using PostgreSQL.
- Analysis of host performance, response times and Superhost status.
- Geographical analysis of accommodation prices, ratings and listing distribution.
- Identification of seasonal trends and potential growth markets.
- Business recommendations to improve host performance, guest satisfaction and market development.

**Tools:** Python, PostgreSQL, Tableau, Data Analytics
